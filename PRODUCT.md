# Straightline Advisors

## Register

Brand. The site is the desk: a family or a shop owner should understand the offer in one minute.

## Users

Indian households and small businesses comparing motor, property, health, and life cover, including people who already hold LIC plans and want them explained.

## Purpose

Show what the desk places, how a policy is chosen, and how a claim is handled. The only action is a callback request.

## Voice

Plain Indian English. Concrete nouns: schedule, nominee, room rent, no-claim bonus, surveyor. No slogans.

## Scene

A couple at a dining table in daylight, comparing a car renewal and a parent's health plan. The page should feel like a finished advisory desk, not a call centre and not a bank poster.

## Dials

- Design variance: 6. Offset layouts, not chaos.
- Motion intensity: 5. One pinned year sequence, small entrances, no loops.
- Visual density: 4.
