# Bima Hub

Indian insurance advisory landing site for motor, health, life including LIC, and shop or home cover.

## Audience

Families and small business owners across India who want a clear quote, a premium estimate, and someone who still answers when a claim opens.

## Core loop

1. Estimate a premium on the calculator, or open the quote form.
2. Compare covers on motor, health, life, and shop or home pages.
3. Request a quote. Preview stores it locally until a live desk is connected.

## Honest preview

No fake IRDAI licence number on the site. Calculator ranges are planning estimates. Quote requests are not emailed until you wire delivery.
